How to connect your Sheet (about 2 minutes, done once)

Google does not let any tool write to a Sheet with only the Sheet's link. A small script, which you add to your own Sheet, receives the rows. It can only add and update rows on a tab called "Applications" in that one Sheet.

  1. Open your Sheet. In its menu, click Extensions, then Apps Script.
  2. Delete whatever is in the editor, then paste the script from the box below. Click the save icon.
  3. Click Deploy, then New deployment. Click the gear icon and choose Web app.
  4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  5. Google asks you to allow it: choose your account, click Advanced, then Go to (project name), then Allow.
  6. Copy the Web app URL, paste it into Connection link above, and click Save settings.

Keep the connection link to yourself: anyone who has it could add rows to that Sheet.
