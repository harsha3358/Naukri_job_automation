Your Google Sheet

Every job the tool applies to is written into a Google Sheet that you own. Here is how to get the link:

  1. Open sheets.new. It creates a blank sheet in your Google account.
  2. Give the sheet a name, for example Naukri applications.
  3. Copy the link from the browser's address bar and paste it below.

One more step lets the tool write to it. After setup, open Settings and follow "How to connect your Sheet". It takes about 2 minutes.
